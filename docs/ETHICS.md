# HushHour Ethics Notes

## Product Position

HushHour is not therapy, diagnosis, or emergency care. It is a time-bounded peer support layer designed to reduce isolation in acute lonely moments.

## Core Ethical Commitments

- Safety before growth
- Human escalation over AI substitution
- Minimal data collection
- No addictive mechanics
- Transparency about limits

## Explicit Boundaries

- No identity graph, follows, or DM carryover
- No streaks, leaderboards, or variable reward loops
- No manipulative urgency messaging
- No emotional scoring or profile inference

## AI Role

AI is used for:

- moderation before message delivery
- crisis signal detection
- metadata-only reflection summarization

AI is not used as:

- therapist replacement
- persuasion engine
- behavioral optimization loop

## Harm Model

Primary risk categories:

- crisis content requiring professional escalation
- harassment/abuse in anonymous interactions
- PII leakage and off-platform solicitation
- repeated overuse/dependence

Mitigations are codified in safety architecture and enforced at message, session, and account levels.
